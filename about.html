ABOUT ME

Vihanga
Sampath.

Creator behind SKYNTH · Sri Lanka

I’m Vihanga Sampath, the creator behind SKYNTH. I enjoy capturing places and moments from a different perspective through aerial photography and cinematic video.

SKYNTH is where I bring together my interest in drone filming, visual creativity and storytelling. My focus is on natural-looking footage, thoughtful composition and visuals that help people experience a place in a new way.

Based in Sri Lanka, I’m interested in projects involving nature, hospitality, weddings, events and creative visual content.

MY CREATIVE TOOLS

The equipment
behind the work.

An overview of the drone, camera equipment and editing software I use for selected SKYNTH projects.

01 · AERIAL PLATFORM

DJI Neo 2

My compact drone platform for aerial photographs and cinematic video perspectives.

02 · DRONE CONTROL

DJI RC-N3

Remote controller used with the drone setup for flight control and precise framing.

03 · ACTION CAMERA

DJI Action 5 Pro

Action camera for ground-level footage and behind-the-scenes moments.

04 · FLIGHT POWER

DJI Neo 2 Batteries

Additional flight batteries from the Fly More Combo for planned shoots and extra flight time.

05 · VIDEO EDITING

Adobe Premiere Pro

My video-editing software for arranging footage, refining sequences and preparing finished films.

06 · PHOTO EDITING

Adobe Photoshop

My photo-editing software for image adjustments, retouching and preparing final photographs.

For the equipment images, use transparent-background PNG files and place them in images/equipment/ with the matching filenames. The image will fit inside the right side of each card.
