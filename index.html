01 — ABOUT ME

Behind

SKYNTH — VISUAL STORYTELLING

I’m Vihanga Sampath, the creator behind SKYNTH. I create visual experiences that turn landscapes, places and moments into stories.

From cinematic aerial footage to carefully composed photographs, I focus on capturing the beauty of Sri Lanka from unique perspectives. My work is driven by a passion for natural, immersive and memorable visuals.

Every project I create is approached with attention to composition, movement, atmosphere and detail — with the goal of turning every frame into a story worth experiencing.
